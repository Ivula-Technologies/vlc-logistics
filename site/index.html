About VLC

A dependable partner for cargo moving through Kenya.

Valence Logistics and Clearing Ltd, trading as VLC Logistics, is a Kenyan logistics and customs clearing company established in 2026. We carry cargo from its point of origin to its final destination: customs brokerage, imports and exports, transit, transshipment, warehousing, local transport and door to door delivery.

We started VLC because importers and exporters deserve a partner that is transparent and quick to respond. In this business, delays and unclear communication turn straight into storage and demurrage charges. We operate from Repen Complex, run our own truck and work with trusted partner transporters, so every consignment gets the right capacity and its delivery date holds.

Our vision

To be the preferred global logistics partner for businesses that move goods through Kenya and across East Africa, recognised for reliability, compliance and service excellence.

Customs clearing & forwarding

We take declarations, duties and port procedures off your desk.

We prepare and lodge accurate documents, classify your goods correctly under the tariff and work with the relevant agencies so your cargo is released without avoidable delay. Mistakes in clearance quickly become storage and demurrage bills, so we treat timing and accuracy as part of the service.

Sea freight

Mombasa Port

Clearance of containerised and conventional cargo, including documentation, duty processing, port charges and release for onward movement.

Air freight

JKIA, Nairobi

Fast clearance of time sensitive imports and exports at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, with close follow up with airlines, handlers and customs.

SGR cargo

ICD Nairobi & Naivasha

Clearance of cargo railed by SGR to the Inland Container Depots, with collection and delivery to the consignee arranged for you.

Documentation

Declarations & processing

Import Declaration Forms (IDF), entries through iCMS and the Kenya TradeNet Single Window, and HS tariff classification of your goods.

Imports

We manage the whole process, from the arrival notice to delivery at your premises.

Exports

We prepare the documents for lawful departure of your goods and coordinate with carriers and shipping lines.

Transshipment

When cargo must continue to another destination, we handle the transfer and its paperwork so it stays compliant with transit rules.

Cargo security and tracking

Real time GPS tracking lets you follow your cargo, and lets us respond quickly to any deviation or delay. Cargo under customs control is handled in line with the Kenya Revenue Authority's Regional Electronic Cargo Tracking System (RECTS).

How a consignment moves with us

Four steps, one contact person.

  1. 01

    Consult and document

    We review the cargo, agree timelines and costs, and collect the documents needed for clearance.

  2. 02

    Clear and comply

    We lodge declarations, classify goods, settle duties and secure release of the cargo.

  3. 03

    Move and track

    Cargo is loaded, secured and moved by road or transit corridor, with GPS tracking and status updates.

  4. 04

    Deliver and confirm

    We deliver to the consignee, confirm the handover and close out the documents.
